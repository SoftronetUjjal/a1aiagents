A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Start with
one workflow.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?+

A chatbot primarily responds to a conversation. An agent can also access approved information, use tools, complete multiple steps and verify the result. Its scope depends on the responsibility it is designed for.

Can an agent work with our existing systems?+

We evaluate your applications, APIs, data sources and security requirements during discovery. The integration approach depends on what each system supports and the reliability the workflow needs.

Do we have to automate everything?+

No. Start with one repetitive or time-consuming workflow, define the boundaries, evaluate the result and expand from there. Human review can remain part of the process.

How do people stay in control?+

Agents can be designed with role-based access, approved data sources, action limits, approval workflows, audit records and escalation rules. The controls should fit the business process.
